Big Homescreen

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Big Homescreen is a simplified Android launcher designed for ease of use. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Information We Access

Location (approximate): Used solely to show local weather on your home screen. Your location is sent to Open-Meteo (a free weather API) to fetch weather data. We do not store or share your location.

App Usage Statistics: If you grant permission, we use Android's Usage Stats to show your most-used apps for quick access. This data stays on your device.

Installed Apps: We read the list of installed apps to display them in the app drawer. This is a core function of any launcher app.

Permissions Explained

Internet: Fetch weather data from Open-Meteo API.

Approximate Location: Determine your area for weather.

Camera: Open the camera app and toggle flashlight.

App Usage Access: Show your most-used apps (optional).

Installed Apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Display all apps in the launcher — required for any home screen app.

Notifications: Send helpful reminders (optional).

Vibrate: Provide haptic feedback for button presses.

Wi-Fi / Bluetooth: Quick toggles in the Tools panel.

Data Storage

All your preferences (pinned apps, settings) are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore. We do not have servers that collect or store your personal data.

Third-Party Services

Open-Meteo (weather): Your approximate location (latitude/longitude) is sent to api.open-meteo.com to retrieve weather data. Open-Meteo is a free, open-source weather API. See their privacy policy at open-meteo.com.

ThinkingData Analytics: We collect anonymous usage data (app installs, session duration, crash reports) to improve app quality. No personal information is collected. See thinkingdata.cn/privacy.

Singular (install attribution): We use Singular to understand how users discover our app. Singular may collect device identifiers for attribution purposes. See singular.net/privacy-policy.

We do not use any advertising networks.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@bigeasyapp.com.
