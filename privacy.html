1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how BIVIO GAME LTD ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Big Homescreen application (the "App"). The App is a home screen launcher for Android designed for ease of use, featuring large text, simplified navigation, weather information, news, and essential tools.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the App, regardless of location. By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, display density, and language/locale settings.
  • App Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the App, such as features used, screens viewed, actions taken (e.g., opening apps, using tools, reading news), session duration, and frequency of use.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Data: Information about app crashes, errors, and performance metrics to help us identify and fix issues.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Your device's advertising identifier (e.g., Google Advertising ID), which may be used to deliver relevant advertisements and measure ad effectiveness. You can reset or opt out of advertising identifiers through your device settings.
  • IP Address: Your IP address may be collected by our service providers in connection with analytics, advertising, and weather services. We use IP-based geolocation to provide localized weather and news content. We do not store your IP address on our own servers.
  • App Configuration Data: Information about your app settings and preferences, such as temperature unit preference, display settings, and notification preferences.

2.2 Information That Requires Your Permission

Certain features of the App require access to device capabilities. We only access these with your explicit permission, and you can revoke permissions at any time through your device settings:

  • Approximate Location (coarse location): Used solely to provide weather information for your area. We do not collect or store precise GPS coordinates.
  • Camera: Used for the magnifier (magnifying glass) and QR code scanner features. Images are processed on your device in real time and are not captured, stored, or transmitted.
  • Notification Access: Used to display notification badge counts on app icons. We only read the count of notifications per app; we do not read the content of your notifications.
  • Usage Statistics: Used to sort apps by frequency of use in the app drawer. This data is processed locally on your device.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or account information
  • Contacts, phone numbers, or call logs
  • Text messages or chat content
  • Photos, videos, or media files
  • Payment or financial information
  • Precise GPS location (we only use approximate/coarse location)
  • Biometric data (fingerprints, face data)
  • Health or fitness data
  • Content of your notifications (only badge counts)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing Core Features: Delivering weather information, news content, app organization, magnifier, QR scanner, flashlight, and other app features.
  • Displaying Advertisements: Showing ads within the App to support our free service. We may use device information and advertising identifiers to serve relevant advertisements.
  • Usage Analytics: Understanding how users interact with the App to improve features, user experience, and performance.
  • Crash Diagnostics: Identifying and fixing bugs, crashes, and performance issues to maintain app stability.
  • Install Attribution: Measuring the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns by understanding how users discover and install the App.
  • Compliance and Security: Complying with legal obligations, protecting against fraud or abuse, and enforcing our Terms of Service.

5. When and With Whom We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with the following categories of service providers who assist us in operating and improving the App:

  • Advertising Networks: We work with advertising partners to display ads within the App. These partners may collect device information and advertising identifiers to serve and measure advertisements.
  • Analytics Services: We use analytics services to understand app usage patterns, feature adoption, and user behavior in aggregate. These services receive device information, usage events, and advertising identifiers.
  • Crash Reporting Services: We use crash reporting services to collect and analyze crash logs, error reports, and device information to maintain app stability.
  • Attribution Services: We use attribution services to measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns by tracking how users discover and install the App. These services may collect advertising identifiers and basic device information.
  • Weather Data Providers: We share approximate location data (latitude and longitude derived from coarse location or IP address) with weather data providers to deliver local weather information.

We may also share information in the following circumstances:

  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, subpoena, court order, or other legal process.
  • Safety and Rights: To protect the safety, rights, or property of our users, ourselves, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, in which case your information may be transferred to the successor entity.

Our service providers are contractually obligated to use your information only for the purposes of providing their services to us and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, and destruction, including:
