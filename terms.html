3. Launcher and Uninstallation

What is a Launcher?

A "launcher" is an Android application that serves as your device's home screen. When you set Big Homescreen as your default launcher, it replaces your device's default home screen experience.

Changing Your Default Launcher

You can change your default launcher at any time:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps (or Apps & notifications).
  3. Tap Default apps (or Choose default apps).
  4. Tap Home app (or Launcher).
  5. Select a different launcher from the list.

The exact steps may vary depending on your device manufacturer and Android version.

Uninstalling Big Homescreen

To uninstall Big Homescreen:

  1. First, switch to a different launcher using the steps above.
  2. Go to Settings > Apps > Big Homescreen.
  3. Tap Uninstall.

Alternatively, you can find Big Homescreen in the Google Play Store and tap Uninstall.

Uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data, including your preferences and settings.
